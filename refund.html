1. Monthly plans

Monthly plans (such as Starter and Growth) are billed in advance and can be cancelled at any time with 15 days' written notice to hello@growthengine.club. Cancellation stops future billing; the current paid month continues until its end date.

If you cancel within 7 days of your first payment and before we have started work (campaign setup or content production), you will receive a full refund. Once work for a billing month has started, fees for that month are non-refundable.

2. One-time setup packages

If you cancel before work begins, you will receive a full refund. If you cancel after work begins, we will refund the amount for work not yet completed, after deducting work already delivered, on a pro-rata basis.

3. Ad spend

Ad spend is paid directly by you to Meta, Google or other platforms and is not collected by us, so it is not refundable by us.

4. Free strategy call

The 30-minute strategy call is free and involves no payment.

5. How refunds are processed

Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 working days to the original payment method through Razorpay. Depending on your bank, it may take a few more days to reflect in your account.

6. Service issues

If you are unhappy with any part of our service, please email us first. We will try to resolve the issue, for example by revising work, before any refund is considered.
